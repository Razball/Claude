# Sprint Deliverables

What the client walks out of the Sprint with.

## Hard deliverables
- A branded Claude workspace (their project) configured to call the AMP MCP server
- A populated Client Module tuned to their business
- Minimum 1 live agent (target 2)
- A live weekly scorecard
- An Optimization Vault (Git-versioned markdown) they own a copy of
- Documented tool-for-job map
- A 90-day action queue based on their time sinks

## Soft deliverables
- Two recorded Sprint sessions they can replay
- A "demo the system" conversation with 2+ concrete wins
- One proactive recommendation already actioned
- Confidence that the system knows their business

## What they don't get
- A generic chatbot
- Unlimited consulting hours
- Custom-coded one-off automations (those come via Managed OS change requests)
- Access to AMP's Protocol Base source

## Managed OS handoff
At Sprint exit, subscription activates. The optimization loop begins running weekly. New automations are scoped in conversation, deployed from the library, and tracked in their vault.
