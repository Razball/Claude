# Discovery Questions (ordered)

The exact question sequence for Session 1. Each question is scored by how much it changes the next question.

## Block A — Orient (5 min)
1. What does your company do, in one sentence?
2. Who's the customer?
3. How big is the team?

## Block B — Pain (10 min)
4. Walk me through your last Monday. What did you personally touch?
5. What's the thing you keep doing that you swore you'd delegate?
6. Where in the business do you feel blind?

## Block C — Offer & Unit Economics (15 min)
7. What's your average order value?
8. What's your blended CAC?
9. What's the margin on a typical order?
10. What's the main objection you hear in the sales process?

## Block D — Funnel & Tools (20 min)
11. Where do leads come from? (break by %)
12. What's the sales motion?
13. List every tool you pay for.
14. Which tool is the source of truth for customer records?
15. Which tool do you personally open every day?

## Block E — Voice (15 min)
16. Paste 3 pieces of copy that worked.
17. What phrases do you ban?
18. What's your sign-off style?

## Block F — Metrics (10 min)
19. What's the one number you check first every week?
20. What do you not measure that you wish you did?

## Block G — 90-day aim (10 min)
21. If this engagement succeeds, what's different in 90 days?
22. What would count as a miss?

## Block H — Wrap
- Summarize what was heard.
- Surface top 3 time sinks back to owner for confirmation.
- Log unknowns in priority order.
